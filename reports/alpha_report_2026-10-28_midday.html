5. Genmab (GMAB): a Phase 3 survival win, and a read-through to Bicara (BCAX)
Move: GMAB +29.3% at 12:01, up steadily from +16.3% at 10:02 and +26.1% at 11:00. BCAX −42.4% to −43.4%, with no company news.
Thesis: At the first interim analysis of the Phase 3 LiGeR-HN1 trial, petosemtamab plus pembrolizumab cut the risk of death by 50% (hazard ratio 0.50; 95% CI 0.37–0.67; p<0.0001) vs. pembrolizumab alone in first-line PD-L1-positive recurrent or metastatic head and neck cancer. The odds ratio for objective response was 3.17, and Genmab says the benefit held regardless of HPV status. It plans to approach regulators and present full results at a future medical meeting. A gain that kept building through the session suggests investors are repricing the asset rather than reacting to a headline (our read). Bicara is developing a competing drug for the same first-line setting with pembrolizumab (background, not re-verified today), so its slide looks like competitive read-through (our inference; Seeking Alpha noted the two moves together).
Evidence: Genmab IR release; StockTitan (timeline and moves); Seeking Alpha (search summary); Investing.com chip (BCAX).
Risks / what could reverse it into the close: These are interim topline results. Absolute medians, response rates and safety details have not been disclosed, and full data could disappoint. For BCAX, the read-through is our inference, and a 40%+ no-news drop can rebound sharply.
Confidence: Medium for GMAB holding most of its gain; Low for BCAX
