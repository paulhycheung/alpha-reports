1. Marvell (MRVL) −5.7% / Lumentum (LITE) −10.6% to −11%: the deal spread blew out after the bell

Move: MRVL went from −3.6% pre-market to −5.7% (AP), about $185. LITE went from −6.8% to −11% (AP) and −10.55% at $751.98 (~11:10). Both moves extended after the open.

Thesis: The deal spread is the signal, and it is getting wider. At 4.5 MRVL shares per LITE share, the implied value is now ~4.5 × $185.2 ≈ $833, against LITE at ~$748–752. That is a gap of ~11%, up from ~5% at Friday's close and ~8.7% at the pre-market snapshot. The implied value is also ~26% below the $1,132 headline price, because MRVL has fallen ~26% since the Oct. 28 unaffected date. Merger-arb trading usually pushes the acquirer down and the target up. Here the target is falling faster, which looks like Lumentum holders selling rather than accepting long-dated Marvell stock (closing is not expected until H1 2028). As of mid-morning the move looks like it has legs, not a fade: both names extended from pre-market into 11 AM, and nothing appeared to stop the selling. A narrowing spread later in the session would be the first sign that arbitrage capital is stepping in.

Evidence: AP via NewsLooks (MRVL −5.7%, LITE −11%). 24/7 Wall St. (MRVL $190.43 −3% and LITE $788.51 −6% early; terms; Lumentum FQ1 revenue $1.26B vs. $1.25B; a co-packaged optics win backed by purchase commitments of more than $5B; Marvell held its Q3 revenue midpoint at $3.15B). Search summary of Benzinga and rallies.ai (Bernstein: the strategy fits but synergies are not yet clear; Morningstar: Marvell may be overpaying; termination fees of $5.8B owed by Marvell and $2.9B owed by Lumentum). Spread arithmetic uses Friday's MRVL close of $196.40 and a LITE close of ~$841, backed out of the −10.55% / $751.98 quote.
