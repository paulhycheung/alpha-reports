In Newsquawk's analysis, rhetoric alone has historically faded, while formal rule changes are what shift royalty revenue.
Evidence: StockAnalysis (CBOE) quote with range and volume; Yahoo losers list (~1:46); Newsquawk headline and analysis; TipRanks (2025 precedent); Seeking Alpha and StockStory (search summaries); TheStreet midday movers (FICO −14.49%; screenshot ≈12:27 PM ET by our time-zone inference).
