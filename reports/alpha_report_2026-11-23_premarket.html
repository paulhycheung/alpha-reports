NVDA — NVIDIA  +7 to +9% pre-market  Confidence: Medium

Thesis: NVDA is the single stock driving today's rebound. Washington has moved from pushing Huang out to publicly backing his return. On Sunday Trump wrote: "I hope the Board of Nvidia rehires Jensen Huang. It would be the best thing for the Company, and also, for the U.S.A." Bessent said "Jensen is on the outside now", which is softer than his earlier demand for an outside CEO. He also drew a line between selling compute to China and supplying the Chinese military. After a 9-of-10-day losing streak, NVDA's longest weekly losing run since 2008 and ~$2.7T of lost market cap, a policy-driven catalyst on top of heavy short positioning could produce a strong opening squeeze.

Evidence: $136.44 pre-market vs. a $125.53 close (Yahoo Finance). Kalshi odds of Huang's return are 16%, up from under 10%. Goldman notes last week's shorting was the largest since April 2025. Goldman's Pasquariello thinks "the high in volatility has been seen for this episode."

Risks / what could fade it: Goldman's Privorotsky says this is a rhetorical pivot, not regulatory relief: the compliance investigations remain open, and the real test is whether licences for offshore data centers are granted. He also says that if NVDA rallies while neocloud credit spreads stay wide, the move is probably just short covering. JPMorgan's 2018 comparison, when the late-year bounce was sold, also applies. A gap of this size on a single social-media post can retrace sharply in the first hour.

Thesis: ZS now trades as a function of CRWD's share price. The deal is 1.135 CRWD shares per ZS share, valued at $260.41 at Friday's prices (a 34% premium). With CRWD down ~9%, the implied value is about $237. ZS at +13% to +18% (about $220–229, from an implied Friday close of ~$194) leaves an arbitrage spread of roughly 3–8%. The size of that spread shows how much regulatory risk the market sees in a 9–14-month closing window. For CRWD, the question is whether the 15.9% dilution is fairly priced against $1.5B of targeted synergies.

Evidence: Combined ARR is about $10.1B (CRWD $6.26B, ZS $3.88B). Synergy targets are $1B of revenue within 30 months and $500M of cost savings within 18–24 months. Holders of about 34% of ZS shares, including founder Chaudhry, support the deal. Closing is expected in the second half of FY28 (about Aug 2027–Jan 2028). Both companies also reported quarters: CRWD revenue +28%, ZS revenue +20%. Peers FTNT, S, NET and OKTA are higher pre-market. JPMorgan names security software as one of the "safest areas within tech."

Risks: Antitrust review of a top-tier endpoint plus network-security combination. CRWD weakness feeds directly into ZS's deal value. Integration and execution risk. A possible rival bid for ZS would widen the spread the other way.
