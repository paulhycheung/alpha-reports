1. How the Open Traded

Stocks opened a little higher, slipped back, then climbed to session highs after a big beat on consumer confidence. Tech and AI hardware led, while Boeing, refiners and Xylem fell hard. 1. Boeing (BA): from flat pre-market to −9.7% and through its 52-week low, the morning's biggest pre-market-to-open reversal
Move: +0.4% pre-market ($188.94 at 7:58, TheStreet), −1.35% ($185.70) at 9:56, about −9% by 10:52 ($171.23, 24/7 Wall St.) and −9.7% ($170.05) at 11:07. Volume was 14.5M shares, ~2.1× its average full day. The stock is ~3.8% below its prior 52-week low of $176.77 (our arithmetic).
Thesis: Into midday this looks more like a continuing repricing than a quick fade, and it is specific to Boeing: GE Aerospace was +2%, RTX was roughly flat and the ITA ETF was only −0.4% to −1%. The headline numbers were mixed. Revenue was $24.19B (+4%), ahead of the ~$23.84B consensus most outlets cite, and the core loss was $0.34 against ~$0.20–0.21 expected, a second straight quarterly EPS miss. The damage is in Commercial Airplanes: segment revenue fell 3% to $10.72B and operating margin dropped to −6.5% from −2.7% in Q2. Boeing blamed delivery mix, higher supply-chain cost estimates and higher projected costs for future production-rate changes, and engine delivery problems are slowing the MAX and 787 ramps. CFRA says the margin deterioration "introduces uncertainty around the recovery pace." The selling accelerated after ~10:00, but we found no specific new headline in that window.
Evidence: Benzinga (segment detail; 161 deliveries; 300 net orders; 737 still working toward a stable 47/month; first 777X delivery in 2027; CEO Kelly Ortberg told CNBC uncertainty is "narrowing"). 24/7 Wall St. (−9%, peer moves, engine-supply commentary). Yahoo earnings blog (9:56 quote, CFRA note). Yahoo most active (11:07 price and volume).
Risks / what could flip it: Free cash flow was positive at $464M, even after ~$700M of DOJ settlement payments. The backlog is a record $745B (more than 6,300 commercial jets), the defense margin rose to 6.2% and Boeing won the F/A-XX fighter award. Climactic volume often marks a short-term low, and a move back above ~$176.77 would argue the selloff was overdone. Sources also disagree on 777X: Yahoo cites a ~$700M charge, while the release table (StockTitan search summary) shows $86M of 777X/767 losses. That remains unreconciled.
Confidence: Medium for continuation into midday; Low for direction over several days

3. Meta (META): the $625 offer price broke briefly and held, and a report says AMD is taking $1B of the deal
Move: −0.3% to −0.5% pre-market, then a low print of $624.41 (−0.98%) at 10:38, 0.09% below the $625 offer (our arithmetic). Thesis: So far this is constructive for the offer price as a floor: the first break below $625 was bought. New since the pre-market check, PANews reports from "market sources" that AMD will invest $1B in the offering, alongside Nvidia's $3B. Meta had said its chip suppliers Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom expressed interest in buying up to $5B of shares in total (Yahoo). PANews also carried a headline that the $25B common offering was about five times oversubscribed; we did not open it. Against that, META is trailing the Nasdaq by ~1.5 points, so the extra share supply is weighing on it.
Evidence: Yahoo most-active snapshots at 10:38 and 11:07 and the AAPL page's "People also watch" panel at ~11:01. PANews (AMD report). Yahoo live blog (suppliers' $5B indication, Nvidia's $3B). Pre-market edition (deal terms).
Risks: Repeated tests of $625 would weaken it as support. More supply is coming from forward sales and a planned ~$60B at-the-market share-sale program starting in 2027. The AMD and oversubscription items are unverified. Critics of suppliers funding their own customers have a growing list of examples. Microsoft's and Alphabet's capex comments after Wednesday's close can move the whole AI-spending complex.
