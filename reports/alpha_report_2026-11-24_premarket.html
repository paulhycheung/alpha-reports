Tone: a "Turnaround Tuesday" test of a short-covering rally. Three things are weighing on AI stocks this morning. (1) Nvidia is down about 5.5% after its 31.5% surge. That is pulling CoreWeave, IREN and Nebius lower. (3) Rates: after Monday's rally, traders priced about a 95% chance of a 25 bp hike on Dec. 9 and about 5% for 50 bp (Yahoo). Two-year yields rose about 8 bp Monday to roughly 4.70%. Outside AI, the tape is healthier: Dollar Tree (+10.8%), Insmed (+14.3% on a $41B Sanofi deal), Fluence (+16.5%), Agilent (+5.7%) and Best Buy (+5%) are all higher on company news.
