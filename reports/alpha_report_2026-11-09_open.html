AMD (+10–11%), Applied Materials (+10%), Monolithic Power (+13%), Broadcom (+5–7%) and the optical names (Coherent +13%, Lumentum +11%) all moved far beyond their pre-market levels. The Nasdaq-100 is now beating the Dow by about 1.5 points. Nvidia itself opened worse than pre-market (about −7%, low $170.24) and was then bought back to about −4%. In short, the money is rotating within AI hardware, away from Nvidia's key-person risk, rather than out of AI. Platforms (META, AMZN) also extended their gains on JPMorgan's focus-list change. Oil-sensitive cyclicals (airlines +6–7%) rallied while energy producers lagged.
