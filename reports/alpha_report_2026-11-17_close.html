1. NVDA: a clean beat and a $130B guide into a heavily de-risked stock (closed −0.44% after −2.55% at 3:10 PM)

Thesis: On the numbers, the quarter cleared every bar. The questions for Wednesday's session are whether the guide is as strong as it looks, and whether the margin and leadership questions outweigh it.

  • Revenue was $115.8B (+20% q/q, +103% y/y), vs. a $108B ±2% company guide and ~$109B consensus.
  • Data Center revenue was $107.6B (+110% y/y).
  • Non-GAAP EPS was $2.68 vs. $2.47 expected. Non-GAAP gross margin was 74.9%.
  • FQ4 guide is $130.0B ±3%, above the ~$124.7B consensus cited in a preview, but the quarter includes an extra week. Our rough per-week math puts the like-for-like guide near ~$120.7B (130 × 13/14), about +4% q/q, vs. +20% this quarter. Yahoo also reported the Street's "whisper" was about $130B, so the headline guide met the whisper rather than beating it.
  • Margin step-down: FQ4 gross margin is guided to 71–72%, ~300 bp below FQ3. That is consistent with an early-ramp drag from Vera Rubin, which Nvidia calls its "fastest product ramp" ever.
  • China: The outlook assumes zero China Data Center compute revenue, so the new remote-access rules don't threaten the guide.
  • Capital return: $28.1B went back to shareholders in the quarter, with $227.3B of buyback authorization left.

Positioning looks extremely washed out: the stock was down ~24–28% in November into the print, and the tech sector's forward P/E is the lowest since 2020. That means a solid print could set off an outsized squeeze, as the late-day short covering already hinted.

Evidence: Nvidia press release (GlobeNewswire, Nov 17). Yahoo live-blog headline. Yahoo's "$130B" whisper and ~7% implied-move article (headline and summary only). StockTitan close −0.44% (via search). Afternoon-edition prints.
