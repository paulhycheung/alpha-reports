The opening question is whether the stock holds below −9.77% (a record, per CNBC) or dip-buyers treat the one-off items as noise.

Evidence: Reuters via Yahoo (−10.2%, ~$400B value at risk, investor quote that hyperscalers no longer get a "free pass"); TipRanks (revenue, capex $51.36B, FCF); Investing.com transcript and CNBC via search summary (EC fine, inventory build, 2027 capex); BigGo/search summary ($8.2B unrealized SpaceX loss in other income).
