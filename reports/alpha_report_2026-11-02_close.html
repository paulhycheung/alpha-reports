1. AMD (AMD) −7.52% to $466.84: most of the implied earnings move is already gone before tomorrow's print

Move: ~$516 pre-market (+2.2%) → $489 (−3%) at 12:49 PM → $473.58 (−6.2%) at ~3:10 → $466.84 (−7.52%) at the close on 31.4M shares. That follows an ~11% drop on Friday. The peak-to-close swing today was ~−9.5%.

Thesis: Options priced ~±8.8% for Tuesday's after-close report (EarningsWatcher, as of Oct. 30; afternoon-edition sources had ~8.4–9%). The stock has already used about that much range on the downside in a day and a half. The afternoon edition's read was that AMD is trading with the AI-infrastructure basket (MU, ALAB, neoclouds), not just on Intel's PC warning, and the close supports that: AMD extended its drop in the final hour along with MU and NVDA while Intel barely moved. Consensus Q3 revenue is ~$13.0B (+42%) and EPS ~$1.94 (TipRanks via the pre-market edition). The swing factor is the Q4 guide against ~$16.2B implied by full-year consensus, plus data-center commentary (Data Center was $6.72B, 58% of sales, last quarter). Worth investigating: whether two days of de-risking have lowered the bar enough that an in-line guide gets a better reception than Friday's tape implies.

Evidence: Investing.com close tables; afternoon edition (24/7 Wall St. segment data and valuation; FX Leaders/FinanceFeeds pre-market price); EarningsWatcher implied moves; Yahoo (AMD reports Tuesday after the bell following Intel's weak outlook).

Risks / what could change it overnight: Apple's memory-cost and PC-demand commentary tonight; any midterm-driven moves in futures; Arista and Astera Labs report the same evening, so read-through will be noisy. A weak Q4 guide could extend the slide well past the implied move, as Intel's did.

Confidence: Low (binary, pre-report)

2. Marvell (MRVL) −12.72% / Lumentum (LITE) −18.61%: the deal spread blew back out to ~12.7% into the bell

Move: MRVL closed at $171.41, slightly above its ~3:10 low ($170.05). LITE closed at $684.27, a new session low (it was $696.48 at ~3:10). At 4.5 MRVL shares per LITE share, the implied value is 4.5 × $171.41 ≈ $771, so the gross spread is ~12.7%. That is up from ~5.1% at Friday's close, ~8.7% pre-market, ~11.7% at 11:29 AM, ~12.2% at 1:01 PM and ~9.9% at ~3:10 PM. The implied value is now ~32% below the $1,132 headline, and MRVL is ~−32% from its Oct. 28 close of $251.65.

Thesis: The afternoon edition read the 3 PM narrowing as merger-arbitrage buyers stepping into LITE. That support disappeared in the final hour. Lumentum holders kept selling while Marvell found a small bid, which looks like holders refusing long-dated Marvell paper (close expected in H1 2028, two shareholder votes and antitrust reviews) rather than arbitrage capital setting the price. Arb buying of LITE is normally hedged by shorting MRVL, so a spread that stays this wide also suggests arb hedging is not what is pressuring MRVL; the selling there looks like outright repricing of the acquirer. Upcoming catalysts in the optical chain: Fabrinet reports tonight (implied ~±21%), Coherent Wednesday after the close, and Lumentum's own report Thursday after the close (implied ~±13%), its first since the deal.

Evidence: Investing.com close tables (prices, volumes); TradingKey close wrap (MRVL −12.72%, LITE −18.59%, 4.5 ratio, 67/33 ownership); StockTitan (Oct. 28 MRVL close, $1,132 headline); EarningsWatcher (FN, COHR, LITE dates and implied moves). Spread math is this report's arithmetic on closing prints.

Risks: Closing prints from one table may not be perfectly synchronous (±0.5 pt on the spread). A semis rebound would lift both legs. Proxy-fight or revised-terms headlines would reset the spread entirely. Optical peers' results (FN tonight) could move LITE independently of the deal.

Confidence: Medium (spread stays wide, 10%+) · Low (direction of either leg)
