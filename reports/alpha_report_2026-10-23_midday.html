2. Towers vs. carriers: CCI and AMT fading, T making lows
Move: CCI +9.6% ($86.37, ~1:13), down from +15.5% at the open and +11.7% at 11:47, now ~5.2% below its opening print. AMT +5.1% (11:47), down from +10.6% at the open (≈ −5.0% from open). T −6.0% ($21.36, ~1:13), its weakest reading of the day, on 46.4M shares (≈1.06× a full day). VZ −1.4%, after −4.6% early.
Thesis: The two legs of the trade are diverging. Tower gains are being steadily sold into: CCI had already traded ≈2.1× its full-day volume by 11:47 while sliding from the open. AT&T, meanwhile, keeps making lows. The market appears to find the SpaceX report more credible as a competitive threat to carriers than as a revenue windfall for towers. One reason worth investigating: AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile were expected to generate nearly 90% of Crown Castle's 2025 site-rental revenue (TIKR, on the earlier 800 MHz spectrum deal). A stronger fourth carrier that squeezes those tenants is a mixed blessing for towers. The open edition's "consolidation rather than extension" read has played out.
Evidence: The Bloomberg report, via Yahoo Finance and TheStreet, says SpaceX is eyeing ≥80K US sites and is in talks with CCI and AMT. Bernstein's Laurent Yoon argued a commitment of that scale would signal far more than spectrum buying alone (Yahoo). Bernstein noted earlier that spectrum purchases do not commit SpaceX to towers (TIKR).
Risks: No leases have been signed, and any SpaceX confirmation or denial could whipsaw both legs. Near-term reports: VZ (Mon Oct 26), AMT (Oct 27), TMUS (Oct 28). Carriers are defensive and could catch a bid if the tape weakens further.
Confidence: Medium
